# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making **AI Study Assistant** a welcoming, respectful, and productive community for everyone who wants to learn, build, and contribute.

This project is a learning environment as well as an open-source software project. We expect contributors to treat each other with respect regardless of their experience level.

---

## Expected Behavior

Contributors are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Communicate constructively.
- Give and receive feedback in good faith.
- Help beginners learn rather than discourage them.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and technical decisions—not people.
- Respect different opinions and approaches.
- Give appropriate credit to other contributors.
- Follow the project's contribution guidelines.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and correct them when possible.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, bullying, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Discriminatory or hateful comments.
- Threats or deliberately hostile behavior.
- Sexual harassment or inappropriate sexual content.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting project discussions or development.
- Trolling or repeatedly provoking other contributors.
- Plagiarism or claiming someone else's work as your own.
- Submitting malicious code or intentionally introducing vulnerabilities.
- Misusing project resources or access.

---

## Technical Disagreements

Technical disagreement is completely normal in software development.

You are encouraged to challenge ideas, architectures, implementations, and technical decisions.

However:

> **Challenge the idea, not the person.**

Good:

> "I think this approach will create performance problems because..."

Not acceptable:

> "You clearly don't know what you're doing."

Strong technical debate is welcome. Personal hostility is not.

---

## Learning Environment

AI Study Assistant is intended to help contributors develop real engineering skills.

Experienced contributors are encouraged to help beginners understand concepts rather than simply dismissing their work.

Beginners are also expected to make a genuine effort to learn, research, test, and improve.

Nobody is expected to know everything.

---

## AI Usage

The project has a separate **AI Usage Policy** defined in `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

In general:

- Using AI to understand concepts, documentation, errors, or technical ideas is allowed.
- AI-generated code should not be submitted as your own contribution.
- Contributors must understand and take responsibility for the code they submit.

Please read `CONTRIBUTING.md` for the complete policy.

---

## Reporting Problems

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report it to the project maintainers.

When reporting an issue, provide:

- A description of what happened.
- Relevant screenshots or messages when appropriate.
- The GitHub issue, Pull Request, or discussion where it occurred.
- Any other information that may help the maintainers understand the situation.

Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take appropriate action when this Code of Conduct is violated.

Depending on the situation, actions may include:

1. Informal warning.
2. Formal warning.
3. Removal of inappropriate content.
4. Temporary restriction from project discussions.
5. Temporary suspension of contribution privileges.
6. Removal from the project or community in serious cases.

The response will depend on the severity and circumstances of the violation.

---

## Good Faith

This Code of Conduct exists to protect the quality of the community, not to prevent honest disagreement.

Maintainers should apply these guidelines fairly and in good faith.

If a situation is unclear, maintainers should prioritize:

- Safety
- Respect
- Fairness
- The long-term health of the project

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories
- Issues
- Pull Requests
- Discussions
- Project-related community groups
- Other spaces where members are representing or participating in the project

---

## Final Principle

We are here to **learn, build, and improve together**.

Be disciplined.  
Be respectful.  
Ask questions.  
Help others improve.  
Argue with ideas, not people.

**Build something worth contributing to—and make the community worth returning to.**
